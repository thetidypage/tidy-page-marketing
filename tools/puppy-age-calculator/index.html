    How the weeks are counted

    The page counts every calendar day from the date of birth up to today on this phone or computer, then divides by 7. Leftover days stay as days. Nothing is rounded up to the next week.

    The home date is separate. It starts a 12-week counter that many owners use as a settling-in window. Week 1 begins on the home date, which counts as day 1. Week 12 is days 78 to 84. The next day, that window is complete.

    A calendar age appears once your puppy is at least a month old. Calendar months are not exactly four weeks, so that line will not always match the week count. The date of birth is repeated in the result so you can see how it was read.

    After this page has loaded, the counting works offline. It does not need an account.

    Questions

    How is a puppy's age in weeks calculated?

    The page counts whole calendar days from the date of birth to today on this device, then divides by 7. Remaining days are shown as days. It does not round up. A puppy born 56 days ago is 8 weeks and 0 days. The time of birth is not used. If you are not sure of the exact day, the result is only as accurate as the date you enter. Once the puppy is at least one calendar month old, a calendar age is shown too. A calendar month is not exactly four weeks, so the two ages will not always match.

    What is the first 12 weeks at home?

    It is a planning window that starts on the collection or home date you enter. The home date counts as day 1. Week 1 is days 1 to 7, and week 12 is days 78 to 84. The next day, the first 12 weeks are complete. Leave the home date blank and the page only shows age from birth. It is not a training plan and it is not medical advice.

    Does this say when to vaccinate, when to worm, or how much to feed?

    No. It does not give vaccination dates, dosing, or feeding amounts. The short notes are ordinary things owners often plan around an age, such as registering with a vet. General guidance only. Always follow your vet's advice.

    Is my puppy's date of birth stored?

    No. The dates stay in the browser tab. This page does not set cookies, does not use analytics, and does not send what you type to a server. Closing the tab clears it. There is no account.

    Why is it called a UK calculator?

    Dates are written day, month, year, and the wording is for people bringing a puppy home in the UK. The arithmetic is the number of calendar days between two dates, so the same count works in any country. It does not use a separate UK formula.
